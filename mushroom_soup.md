---
title: Mushroom Soup
---

Fill a pot with (finely chopping all):

- 230g white mushrooms
- 100g shittake mushrooms
- half a white onion
- 6 cloves of garlic
- half a thumb of ginger

Put a small amount of oil (aesthetic). The contents of the pot has enough water
to prevent burning. Cook on medium heat.

Then add:

- 200ml beef broth
- 200ml coconut milk (OR, just the same volume of more beef broth)
- soy sauce
- thyme
- sesame oil
- black pepper

Optionally thicken by shaking 10g flour and 20g of water in a mason jar then
adding the slurry (then simmering for a few minutes).

Optionally add black fungus strips (also requires simmering after).
