---
title: Gary's Thai Coconut Chicken
---

## Rice
Prepare jasmine rice. Get 1 and 1/2 cups.
- wash the rice
- cook in a pot or rice cooker. start with twice as much water as rice and low boil until most of the water boils away.

Serve the chicken on the rice.

## Chicken

Get 12 boneless skinless chicken thighs.

Prepare the rub:
- 1 tablespoon paprika and cumin, each
- 1/2 teaspoon pepper and salt, each

Rub the chicken with the rub, applying evenly. Then set it aside for 20 minutes (or less if impatient). Then, lightly brown the chicken in a thin layer of oil at low heat.

Take the chicken out of the oil and set aside.  Finely chop 3 stalks of green onion, a 2-inch nub of ginger, and 2 cloves of garlic, cook it for a short while in the same oil.

Add to the chicken:
- 1 tablespoon of chili sauce
- 3 tablespoons of oyster sauce
- 2 tablespoons of molasses
- 400ml of coconut milk

Combine all ingredients in the pot with the oil. Low simmer uncovered for 20 minutes (or as needed to thicken).
