---
title: Veggie Wrap
---

Make the sauce. Mix:

- 1/8 cup of ranch dressing
- 1/2 tbsp mesquite seasoning

Prepare the chickpeas:

- roast in the oven on a parchment paper on a baking sheet. roughly 400f for
  20min
- cook until mildly crunchy

Place in a tortilla wrap in even portions:

- the sauce
- the roasted chickpeas
- shredded
    - cheese
    - carrot
