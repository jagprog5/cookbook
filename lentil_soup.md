---
title: Lentil Soup
---

Cook in a rice cooker:

- 1 cup red lentils (wash the lentils first!! then, add to the rice cooker with 2
  cups of water)
- 1 chopped carrot
- 1 chopped red pepper

Add half a cooked sweet potato (skin removed).

Add liquids:
 - 450ml of carton chicken broth.
 - 200ml of coconut milk.

Blend with a hand blender.

Add seasoning:
- 1 teaspoon [curry powder](https://www.bulkbarn.ca/en/Products/All/Curry-Powder-904).
- 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon. Mix and enjoy.
