---
title: Breaded Chicken
---

Create the breading. Good for around 10 chicken legs, or 10 chicken thighs.

Mix in a bowl. Follow only one of the following options:

## OPTION 1

- 30g breadcrumbs
- 20g parmesan cheese finely grated
- 5g oregano
- 5g onion powder
- (optional) butter

## OPTION 2

- 30g breadcrumbs
- 5g parmesan cheese finely grated
- half a lemon's juice
- 10g olive oil
- 10g balsamic vinegar
- 1 pinch seasoned salt

---

Dab the raw chicken in the breading to cover it. Bake at 400f 30 min.
